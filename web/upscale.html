SeedVR2 uses no text encoder — only the diffusion model and its VAE.

Black output? Known with the plain 7B FP8 model (NaN latents) — switch to the INT8 convrot or 7B Sharp FP8 variant from the Model downloads window.

The upscaler processes the image in chunks (tiles). If you run out of VRAM, lower the tile size — 1024 works well on ~14.5 GB VRAM, drop to 512 if needed.

Upscaled images are saved to your custom folder as well as the ComfyUI output folder.
